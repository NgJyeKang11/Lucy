How to use
  • Set volume: curl your middle, ring and pinky fingers, then pinch or spread your thumb and index finger.
  • Lock a value: hold your fingers still until the ring fills and turns amber. Small movements are then ignored, so you can open your hand safely.
  • Re-adjust while locked: move your fingers clearly further apart or closer together.
  • Mute / unmute: make a fist and hold it for about 1 second. While muted, the volume is frozen; only the fist works.
